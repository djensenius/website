Selected press, interviews, and exhibition coverage about David Jensenius's work in sound, installation, performance, and public art.

## Selected press

### [In the ruins of a burned church, a free Toronto art festival is ready to launch](https://www.cbc.ca/arts/in-the-ruins-of-a-burned-church-a-free-toronto-art-festival-is-ready-to-launch-9.7312941)

_CBC Arts_, Leah Collins, August 19, 2026

A CBC Arts preview of _Where Walls Meet Sky_ at St. Anne's Anglican Church, including _Telephone Booth_: "Dial 1, and you can leave a voice mail. Dial 2, and you'll hear a pre-recorded message inspired by the site."

### [Interactive 'Telephone Booth' installation arrives at Cambridge sculpture garden](https://www.cambridgetoday.ca/local-news/interactive-telephone-booth-installation-arrives-at-cambridge-sculpture-garden-12849702)

_CambridgeToday_, Keegan Kozolanka, October 2, 2026

Feature coverage of _Telephone Booth_ at the Cambridge Sculpture Garden, with interview comments on the phone booth as an object "out of place and out of time."

### [CAFKA celebrates 25 years of art in Kitchener](https://www.communityedition.ca/cafka-celebrates-25-years-of-art-in-kitchener/)

_The Community Edition_, Josh Milton-Bell, September 3, 2026

Coverage of CAFKA's 25th anniversary, noting _Telephone Booth_ as one of two historical CAFKA works reconstructed in 2026.

### [M(app)ing All the Sound](https://www.communityedition.ca/mapping-all-the-sound/)

_The Community Edition_, Kurtis Thomas, March 3, 2016

A profile of _FoundSounds_, described as an ongoing art project for recording, mapping, sharing, and walking with everyday sounds.

### [FoundSounds app release version 2](https://www.thewire.co.uk/news/39979/foundsounds-app-release-version2)

_The Wire_, January 12, 2016

International sound-art coverage of the second version of _FoundSounds_, a field-recording and sound-walk app.

### [Making Waves: Udo Noll and David Jensenius](https://wavefarm.org/wf/archive/ccxc3f)

_Wave Farm / NAISA_, Bekah Simms and Darren Copeland, July 9, 2016

Radio interview on locative soundscape apps, pairing _FoundSounds_ with Udo Noll's Radio Aporee in advance of World Listening Day.

### [St. Anne's art exhibition finds rebirth and new life amid church ruins](https://anglicanjournal.com/st-annes-art-exhibition-finds-rebirth-and-new-life-amid-church-ruins/)

_Anglican Journal_, Matthew Puddister, September 10, 2026

Supporting coverage of _Where Walls Meet Sky_ with documentary context for _Telephone Booth_ inside St. Anne's Anglican Church.
